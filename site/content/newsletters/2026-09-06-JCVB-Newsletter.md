1. **🏐 Patriots Split the Preseason with a Dominant 3-0 Win over Bel Air** - The Patriots closed out the preseason Friday with their first win, a 3-0 scrimmage over Bel Air — 25-10, 25-19, 25-12 — and everyone who stepped on the floor got into the result.
	- **Jackson DeReamer** had an outstanding afternoon on the outside, 14 of the team's 32 kills, with **Peyton Williams**, **Julian Young**, and **Luke Boyle** all adding to the attack.
	- Setters **Mason Proctor**, **Luke Boyle**, and **Gregg Wisniewski**, combined for 26 assists to run the offense. **Proctor** also led the team with 4 aces.
	- The passing was the story underneath all of it — **Andrew Hall** and **Tyler Olivi** were the steadiest in the back court, combining for a Serve Receive Rating of 2.3.
	- **Peyton Williams** led the defense with six digs to go along with his 5 kills as an Outside Hitter.
	- 📊 [Full recap, box score, and set-by-set breakdown](https://sites.anvilor.com/jcvb/games/2026-09-04-bel-air/)
